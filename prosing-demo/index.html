The project

About ProSing

ProSing applies specified singing techniques to selected regions of an existing recording. It aims to preserve the lyrics, underlying melody, timing, and singer identity while changing how the phrase is sung.

The framework adapts a pretrained flow-matching acoustic model using paired technique data. Frame-level technique conditions control vocal timbre, while continuous pitch trajectories specify the extent and evolution of vibrato and glissando. Different techniques can be applied sequentially or combined in the same region.

Here, zero-shot refers to conversion for source singers unseen during ProSing's task-specific fine-tuning.

The demos

Listening guide

The 16 examples below demonstrate single-technique and multi-technique control in Chinese and English. Both language sets are shown in full on this page.

  1. Listen to Source, then ProSing (ours), to compare the original and converted performances.
  2. Read the instruction above each player. Highlighted lyrics identify locally edited regions; “Entire excerpt” means the technique is requested throughout the clip.
  3. Open Compare with baselines to hear TCSinger and, where available, Vevo without leaving the page.

Headphones are recommended. Playing a new recording pauses the previous one. Pitch-control values describe the requested command, not a measurement of the generated output.
