01 / Overview

Expressive control, grounded in the source.

ProSing applies specified singing techniques to selected regions of an existing recording. The goal is to preserve the lyrics, underlying melody, timing, and singer identity while changing the requested vocal technique.

The model adapts a pretrained flow-matching acoustic model with frame-level technique conditions. Paired training helps it learn to follow explicit technique controls; continuous pitch trajectories provide detailed control over vibrato and glissando.
